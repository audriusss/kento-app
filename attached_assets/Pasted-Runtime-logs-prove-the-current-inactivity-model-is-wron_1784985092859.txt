Runtime logs prove the current inactivity model is wrong for the product.

Evidence:

ERROR_SERVER_DISCONNECTED
infra retry succeeds
onReadyForSpeech occurs
INACTIVITY_WINDOW_PRESERVED remainingMs=-1763
conversation closes immediately with inactivity-timeout

The 30-second timeout must represent 30 seconds of actual idle listening time.

It must NOT be an absolute conversation-session lifetime.

Implement a simple inactivity-budget model.

Required behaviour:

1. Start a fresh 30-second inactivity window when the recognizer becomes ready for the first time.

2. Pause the inactivity countdown while:
- the user is speaking
- speech results are being processed
- Kentas is generating an AI response
- Kentas is speaking through TTS
- SpeechRecognizer is recovering from infrastructure errors such as ERROR_SERVER_DISCONNECTED

3. Infrastructure retry time must not consume the inactivity budget.

4. After a successful meaningful user interaction and Kentas finishes replying:
- start a fresh 30-second inactivity window when listening becomes ready again

5. After ERROR_NO_MATCH caused by silence:
- preserve and resume the remaining inactivity budget
- do not reset to 30 seconds on every NO_MATCH loop

6. If ERROR_NO_MATCH happens after onBeginningOfSpeech but no meaningful text was recognised:
- resume the remaining budget from before speech began
- do not count speaking duration as inactivity

7. Close the conversation only after a total of 30 seconds of actual idle listening time.

Keep the implementation simple.

Recommended state:

private var inactivityRemainingMs = 30_000L
private var inactivityStartedAtMs: Long? = null
private var inactivityJob: Job? = null

Functions:

startOrResumeInactivityCountdown()
pauseInactivityCountdown(reason)
resetInactivityCountdown(reason)
clearInactivityCountdown(reason)

When pausing:
- subtract only elapsed active-listening time from inactivityRemainingMs
- cancel the Job
- do not subtract time spent speaking, processing, TTS, or reconnecting

When resetting after a meaningful completed interaction:
- inactivityRemainingMs = 30_000L

Add tests for:

1. 10 seconds idle + 5 seconds speaking + NO_MATCH:
   20 seconds remain, not 15 and not 30.

2. 10 seconds idle + 4 seconds ERROR_SERVER_DISCONNECTED recovery:
   20 seconds remain after reconnect.

3. Successful user result + Kentas reply:
   next ready state starts with a fresh 30 seconds.

4. Repeated silence NO_MATCH loops:
   countdown eventually expires after 30 seconds total idle listening.

5. Conversation never closes while the user or Kentas is speaking.
